# TrendVista Store Policies

## Festive Exchange Window

Orders placed between 1 October and 15 November can be exchanged until 30 November,
even if the normal 10-day exchange window has passed. Festive orders are not eligible
for cash refunds; they receive store credit instead.

## Beauty Products

Beauty and personal-care products are non-returnable once the seal is broken.
Our Beauty range is sourced from a single supplier in Mumbai; when that supplier is
delayed, Beauty items are marked "Coming soon" and cannot be ordered until restocked,
which typically takes 3-4 weeks.

## Delivery

TrendVista delivers to tier-2 and tier-3 cities in 5-8 business days. Orders above
₹1,499 ship free; smaller orders pay ₹59. Cash on Delivery is available on all orders
up to ₹4,000.

## Loyalty Programme

TrendVista Insiders (customers with 5 or more delivered orders) get 10% off every
order and free express delivery during festive sales.
