# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Order status (Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled, Returned) is visible under
"My Orders". Tracking IDs are emailed once an order is shipped.

**Q: Why was my order cancelled?**
A: Orders are auto-cancelled if payment fails, if the delivery address is
unserviceable, or if all items in the order go out of stock before dispatch.

**Q: Do you restock out-of-stock items?**
A: Yes. Popular Electronics items are typically restocked within 4-6 weeks of
going out of stock, based on supplier lead time.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and Cash on Delivery (with the
restrictions listed in the Shipping Policy).

**Q: How are loyalty/repeat customers identified?**
A: Customers with more than 3 delivered orders in the last 6 months are
tagged as repeat customers and receive early access to sales.
