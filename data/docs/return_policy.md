# Return & Refund Policy

Customers can return any item within 15 days of delivery, provided it is unused
and in its original packaging. Refunds are processed to the original payment
method within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes quality check.

Electronics category items (earbuds, smartwatches, speakers) can only be
returned if the manufacturer seal is intact — opened electronics are eligible
for exchange only, not refund, due to hygiene and warranty-void policies.

Fashion items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

Shipping cost for returns is borne by the customer unless the item arrived
damaged or the wrong item was shipped, in which case return shipping is free.
