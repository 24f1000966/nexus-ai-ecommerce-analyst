# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 4-6 business days and is free on orders above ₹999.
Orders below ₹999 incur a flat ₹49 shipping fee.

Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available in Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru,
Delhi, Hyderabad, and Chennai for a ₹149 surcharge.

Electronics items are shipped with tamper-evident packaging. If Electronics
stock is unavailable, the order is auto-split and the remaining items are
shipped first, with the Electronics item shipped separately once restocked.

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders under ₹5000, except for
Electronics items above ₹3000 in value, which require prepayment.
